# Sealed reservoir in a fractured graben-horst formation

Thesis section: 6.3.4.

Models a sealed reservoir within an idealized graben-horst formation using mortar interfaces for principal faults and EFEM for 26 subsidiary fractures. Pressure decreases during the first year and returns to its initial value during the second. Simulations with and without subsidiary fractures assess their influence on deformation and fault slip.
