# Dirichlet boundary condition enforcement

Thesis section: 3.3.2.

Examines approximate Dirichlet enforcement at the boundary of a mortar interface. Conditions are imposed strongly on the mortar side and transferred weakly to the other side, allowing the test to assess boundary-induced multiplier oscillations and the behavior of nodal and P0 multiplier spaces.

Run `main` after `initGReS`. Case-specific inputs and helpers are in `Input/` and `Utils/`. Results are written to `Output/`, whose previous contents are replaced on each run.
