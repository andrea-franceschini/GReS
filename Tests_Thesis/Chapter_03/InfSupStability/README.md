# Lagrange multiplier spaces and inf-sup stability

Thesis section: 3.2.3.1.

Evaluates the discrete inf-sup constant on successively refined nonconforming macroelement meshes. Standard, dual, unstabilized P0, and jump-stabilized P0 multiplier spaces are compared to detect spurious modes and assess whether stability is retained under refinement.

Run `main` after `initGReS`. Case-specific inputs and helpers are in `Input/` and `Utils/`. Results are written to `Output/`, whose previous contents are replaced on each run.
